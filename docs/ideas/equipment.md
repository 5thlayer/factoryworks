# FactoryWorks equipment system

## Armor

1. light
2. heavy
3. power -> energy based
4. tech -> energy based

## Armor module system

similar to Factorio's

- portable solar panel, portable battery, portable burning|fission|fusion reactor
- exo-skeleton (faster walk|run speed)

## Traversal modules

- hoverpack (like satisfactory, no upward movement, only hover)
- jetpack (like S)
- pogo (a super jump with no steering possibility, unless hover/jetpack is also equipped)

## Weapons

- melee weapon
- railgun
- missile
