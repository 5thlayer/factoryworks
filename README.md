# FactoryWorks

A suite of Minecraft 26.1.2 / NeoForge tech mods that brings Factorio's logistics into an ordinary world and mixes with other tech mods. Each mod owns one capability and plays in vanilla.

FactoryWorks is a fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wube Software, the makers of Factorio.

## The suite

The mods live in their own repositories: [Groundworks](https://github.com/5thlayer/groundworks) (placement), [Beltworks](https://github.com/5thlayer/beltworks) (items on belts), [Pipeworks](https://github.com/5thlayer/pipeworks) (fluids), [Wireworks](https://github.com/5thlayer/wireworks) (energy), [Craftworks](https://github.com/5thlayer/craftworks) (crafting machines), [Fieldworks](https://github.com/5thlayer/fieldworks) (resources), [Voidworks](https://github.com/5thlayer/voidworks) (void energy) and [Gearworks](https://github.com/5thlayer/gearworks) (Gear, not yet released). Only Groundworks is required by the others. FactoryWorks ships no modpack of its own: a player puts the mods in whatever pack they like.

## This repository

This is the suite's home: the shared vocabulary, the decisions that touch more than one mod, and the parent issue of every cross-mod topic.

- The suite's vocabulary is in [GLOSSARY.md](https://github.com/5thlayer/factoryworks/blob/main/GLOSSARY.md), its decisions in [docs/adr](https://github.com/5thlayer/factoryworks/tree/main/docs/adr).
- A bug or idea for one mod goes on that mod's issue tracker; one that spans several goes on [this one](https://github.com/5thlayer/factoryworks/issues).
