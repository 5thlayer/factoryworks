## 0.1.0

First release, for the FactoryWorks modpack on NeoForge, Minecraft 26.1.2.

- Stone, Steel and Electric Furnaces, and Burner and Electric Mining Drills.
- Assembling Machines 1–3, the Chemical Plant and the Oil Refinery, each crafting one held recipe at its Factorio rate.
- The Boiler, Offshore Pump and Steam Engine, and the Pumpjack on oil wells.
- Electric poles that power machines in their area without wires, and Accumulators.
- The Radar, charting the map around it.
- Ore patches that hold an amount and run out, and the ore fields and oil wells laid across Terra.
- A placement preview for every machine, Fast Replace, and a building reach of 16 blocks.
